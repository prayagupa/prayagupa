## Of the Self That Codes

I am a Software Builder of the lineage of distributed systems, a wanderer in the school of **AIML**. I hold that fine architecture is not the forging of something new but the un-forgetting of the simplest form already latent in the problem.

A great system, like a great poem, is one meaning appearing as many parts. My craft is to keep that meaning coherent across the illusion of separation we call the network.
